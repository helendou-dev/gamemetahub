## The Early Adopter Bonus: You Have Until September 25

This is the most under-covered fact about the launch. When Capcom moved the date forward on July 2, the digital pre-order bonus was converted into an **Early Adopter Bonus** — and it stays claimable for roughly three weeks *after* launch. You do not have to buy before September 4 to get it.

**Deadline: September 25, 2026 at 4:59 PM PT.** After that, the bonus items are gone (though Capcom notes they "may be made available by other means" later).

Know the distinction: **charms are equipable stat items** that strengthen Miyamoto Musashi (managed on the Equipment screen), while **sword appearances are purely cosmetic** (changed via the in-game Spirit Mirror). Neither the Spirit Mirror nor the Equipment screen is available from minute one — both unlock as you progress through the opening chapters.

## Keep Your Demo Save: The Kubi Akari Charm

If you played the free demo — the Kiyomizu-dera Temple stage capped by a duel with Musashi's rival Sasaki Ganryu, roughly 30 minutes of content — **do not delete that save file**. Demo progress does not transfer, but holding demo save data on your system grants the **Charm: Kubi Akari** in the full game, at zero cost.

The demo is live on PS5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC (Steam and Epic). A Switch 2 demo has not released, so Switch 2 players simply miss this one charm — it's nice, not essential. If you haven't touched the demo yet, it's also the best combat tutorial you'll get before launch: the full game will not slow down much to re-teach parries.

<ProTip title="One Switch 2 Gotcha">
Because the release date moved, Capcom automatically **cancelled all digital pre-orders on Nintendo Switch 2**. If you pre-ordered digitally on Switch 2 before July 2, check your eShop — you must place a new order to keep your place and your Early Adopter Bonus. Physical Switch 2 pre-orders were unaffected; the physical version ships as a game-key card.
</ProTip>

## Your First Hours: Five Combat Fundamentals

Critics agree the combat is the reason this game earned its scores, and the learning curve is real. Five systems to prioritize from the opening chapter:

- **Parry and deflect.** Blocking is a fallback; timed deflections open enemies up for punishment. The demo's Sasaki Ganryu duel is a preview of how demanding late-game parry windows get.
- **Issen finishers.** Counter-attacks timed against enemy strikes trigger the series' signature one-flash executions. Risky, but they restore resources and delete threats instantly.
- **Soul absorption.** Slain Genma release souls. Absorb them — they're your progression currency, and greedily harvesting mid-combat is part of the rhythm.
- **Oni Armament.** The gauntlet's transformed states are your burst damage and armor-break answer. Learn when to commit to it rather than spamming it.
- **Charm loadouts.** The moment the Equipment screen unlocks, slot your Lion Dog (and Kubi Akari if you kept the demo save). Early-game stat curves are where charms matter most.

## Platform Quick Facts

- **PS5** — Listed as PS5 Pro Enhanced; digital and physical editions available.
- **Xbox Series X|S** — Supports Xbox Play Anywhere: one digital purchase covers console and Windows PC.
- **PC** — Steam, Epic Games Store, and Microsoft Store. 50 GB, SSD required; a free benchmark tool is available if you're unsure your rig can handle RE Engine at these settings.
- **Switch 2** — Physical version is a game-key card; digital buyers who pre-ordered before the date change must re-order.

There is no confirmed Game Pass or PS Plus day-one inclusion, so don't plan around a subscription.

## The Bottom Line

Day 1 is simpler than it looks: the game unlocks at 9:00 PM PT on Thursday for West Coast players and midnight local Friday for console players elsewhere; your bonus is safe until September 25 either way; and if you still have demo save data sitting on your console, it's worth a free charm. Buy the Standard edition unless the Deluxe charms genuinely appeal, keep your parry finger loose, and enjoy the first mainline Onimusha in two decades — critics overwhelmingly say it was worth the wait.
