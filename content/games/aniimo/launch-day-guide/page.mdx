## Lunara vs Helion: The Permanent Starter Choice

After the tutorial missions, the story puts two talking Light-type Aniimo in front of you and asks which one you want to save. You pick one — and the other goes to your rival Nico. Beta reporting consistently indicates this choice is **permanent for your character**, so treat it as a playstyle decision, not a coin flip.

The good news: both starters share the exact same base statline (90 HP, 116 ATK, 85 Break, 65 M.DEF / 65 P.DEF, 83 Regen). Neither is strictly stronger. The difference is entirely in how they fight:

Pick **Lunara** if you want a forgiving ranged kit with built-in sustain — boosted basic attacks consume her marks and heal her back, which makes early boss fights much safer. Pick **Helion** if you enjoy close-quarters action; his Holy Storm pulls enemy groups into a kill zone, and his halo-charging loop rewards staying in the fight.

Both evolutions trigger through their Ultimate abilities mid-battle rather than permanently replacing the base form, granting a 15% damage boost for the rest of the fight. Beta testers widely favored Lunara for fast group clears and high-difficulty content, but the safest answer is the control scheme you will enjoy for dozens of hours.

## Your First Two Hours on Idyll

Here is the priority order that beta veterans converge on:

1. **Catch your tutorial Embercub** — the fire-type starter taught during the opening. Do not reroll for it; its value is teaching the capture flow, and it evolves into a solid mid-game fighter.
2. **Make your starter choice deliberately** — knowing the Lunara/Helion split above before the story forces the decision.
3. **Learn Command AND Twine** — Command keeps your human on the field while directing Aniimo; Twine merges you into a creature directly. The tutorial forces both before Nimbus Fields opens, and mid-fact hot-swapping is where the combat depth lives.
4. **Open the Homeland RV as soon as the story offers it** — this is your farm, kitchen, egg hatchery, and offline income track. Every hour you delay it is offline production lost. Queue the longest kitchen recipe you can afford before logging off, and remember that Aniimo assigned to Homeland staff leave your active roster.
5. **Save Aniipod Ultras for rare spawns** — weather-conditional variants like Thunderstorm and Prismana forms appear only during specific weather events, one rare form per event. Do not burn premium capture items on the first common creature you meet.
6. **Try Egg Heist once the queue opens** — the three-player PvEvP mode in the Lost Isles is the fastest way to feel out the combat against real opponents.

## One Caveat Before You Commit

Everything above reflects the final beta build. Pawprint Studio has already changed the evolution system twice between betas, so expect live-tuning in the launch week patch notes. The starter statline and roles have been stable across every test, but exact skill percentages may shift.

Servers open **September 15 at 7:00 PM PT**. Pick your starter before then — and if you are queueing on PC, note that the install wants **45 GB of free space**, with separate server regions for Americas, Europe, and APAC, so choose the region closest to you at first login.
