Aniimo's iOS and Android versions go live on **September 23, 2026**, one week after the PC and console release — and if you already have a Pathfinder on PC, PS5, or Xbox, the mobile client is not a separate game. It is the same account, the same servers, and the same save, which makes launch day equal parts opportunity and trap. Get the account-linking order right and you can farm dailies from your phone and claim 60 free Sparkling Crystals from the Boundless Twining event. Get it wrong and you can end up staring at a level-1 character on the wrong server, wondering where your week of progress went.

This guide covers everything the scattered official announcements don't put in one place: exact mobile unlock times by region, device requirements, the cross-progression and server rules, the email-linking mistake Pawprint Studio itself warns about, how to claim your closed beta test rewards, and every launch-window bonus worth claiming while you are in there. If you are brand new to the game instead, start with our [Aniimo launch day guide](/games/aniimo/launch-day-guide) for the first-two-hours playbook, then come back here for the mobile specifics.

I went through the full account-linking flow on a fresh third-party login when the PC version opened, and the number of ways to accidentally fork your progress is genuinely impressive. The good news: every one of them is avoidable with about five minutes of prep before you tap Install.

## Cross-Progression Rules: Same Account, Same Server

This is the section to read *before* you install, because it contains the only two irreversible-ish decisions in the whole process.

Aniimo shares progression across PC, PS5, Xbox, iOS, and Android, but the sharing happens **within a server**. Data on different servers is completely independent — linking platforms does not merge characters or move progress between regions. So the rule is simple to state:

1. **Same account** — sign in on mobile with the login method attached to your existing Aniimo account (more on how to verify that below).
2. **Same server** — pick the regional server your PC/console character already lives on.

If both match, your character, captured Aniimo, Homeland, and inventory appear on your phone exactly as you left them. The platform changes; the server does not.

The PS5 wrinkle deserves its own warning: **the console's server is assigned based on your PSN account's registered region**. If your PC character is on, say, the Europe server but your PSN account is registered in the US, linking them will not combine the two — they are on different servers and the data stays separate. Check which server your existing character is on before you connect anything.

The other one-time decision is the **first console login with an unused PSN or Microsoft account**. That login triggers the Aniimo Pass linking prompt, and it is the flow for attaching an *existing* Aniimo Pass to that console account. Dismiss it and the process registers a brand-new Aniimo Pass instead, which prevents linking your existing progress the same way later. If you are bringing a console online for the first time this week, do not button-mash past that prompt.

## The Account-Linking Trap: Attach an Email Before You Do Anything Else

Here is the mistake Pawprint Studio has explicitly warned about, and the one I would put on a sticky note: **on the first login with a third-party account (Google, and certain other platform logins), Aniimo shows a one-time "Link Email" screen. If you skip it, that same screen cannot simply be reopened later** — at that point you are looking at customer support to recover the link.

Why does this matter so much? Because the email address is the spine of your account identity across platforms:

- Pre-registration rewards, test rewards, and web event rewards are all distributed **to the in-game account tied to your verified email**.
- Cross-platform sign-ins resolve through that identity. If your mobile login and your PC login point at different identities, the game treats them as two different players — different progress, different rewards, and no Boundless Twining credit (that is the 60-crystal event below).
- One email address **can** be linked to several third-party platform accounts, but each platform account can only ever carry **one** email. Choose an email you actually control long-term.

If you used an email to pre-register, join a test, or enter a web event before launch, enter **that same address** in the linking screen and verify it. That routes every reward you have earned to one account.

There is one friendly exception: the **Steam flow is more forgiving**. If you log in with Steam and skip the email prompt, the official FAQ says you can complete the association later from the in-game **[Email Association]** page. But that leniency does not extend to every third-party first-login flow, and I would not build a plan around finding out which ones are strict. Link the email the first time you are asked, every time.

**My recommended order for launch hour** — I would do exactly this, in this order:

1. On your PC or console, log in and confirm which **server** your character is on.
2. Still on PC/console, open account settings and confirm the email attached to your Aniimo Pass.
3. Install the mobile client, then sign in using a method linked to that same account.
4. **Before creating anything**, confirm your existing character and progress appear.
5. If the mobile client looks like a fresh save — stop. Check the server selection and the sign-in method first. Do not grind an hour on the wrong profile.
6. Only then open the event page and start claiming.

That fifth step is the diagnostic that saves people from themselves: Pawprint has confirmed PC and mobile share data, so a correct sign-in always shows your real character. A blank slate is an account or server problem, not a game bug.

## Closed Beta Test Rewards: What Survives and How to Claim It

Short version: **your test data did not survive, but your money and your participation did.**

All data from previous tests was reset before launch — nothing from the betas carries into the live game. But two separate redemption tracks exist:

**Test participation rewards.** The official test reward redemption page is live on the Aniimo site. Log in on that page **with the account you used during the test**, and you can see and claim your test-exclusive rewards — plus the eggs that the "Bond Promise" Aniimo reunion event has been holding for you. Pawprint's own guidance is to claim soon after servers open rather than letting it sit.

**Test top-up refunds.** If you spent real money during the closed beta, you get it back in Lumin Crystals at tiered rates — **200% of your first tier of test spending, 150% on the next tier, and 110% above that**, per the official top-up event terms — and the claim window runs all the way to **March 15, 2027**. Beta commemorative items arrive by in-game mail. That is a generous conversion by gacha standards, and there is no reason to rush it, but do not forget it exists.

One security note worth repeating because launch week is prime scam season: all in-game rewards are distributed through official events and in-game mail. Pawprint will **never** ask you for a verification code or a payment to release a reward. If a Discord DM or a "redemption site" asks for either, it is a scam.

The event rules are stricter than they look:

- **Both logins must be the same game account.** Signing into mobile with a fresh account while your PC character sits on another identity completes nothing, even if both devices are physically yours.
- **PC and console count as one side.** You do not need to touch every platform — one qualifying desktop-or-console login plus one mobile login is the full requirement.
- **The event unlocks at level 25.** If the event page is not visible on a brand-new character, that is why — keep progressing before assuming the event is missing.
- Rewards are claimed **from the event interface** after both objectives register. Installing the app is not the same as completing the login objective.

If a login does not register, the fix order is: confirm both sign-ins resolve to the same account, confirm the server matches, re-log in fresh after the event window is live on your server, then reopen the event page. And remember the trap from earlier — a third-party login that was never linked to your email is exactly the kind of split identity that makes this event silently fail.

## Every Other Launch Reward Worth Grabbing While You Are In There

Boundless Twining is the flagship, but the launch reward stack is spread across several tracks, and mobile launch day is the natural moment to sweep all of them:

- **Platform linking bonuses** — each platform account you link (email, Google, Facebook, Discord, TikTok, VK, Line) pays out **50 Glimmer and 10,000 Credits**, and linking your email adds **10 Growth Flowers** on top. These are permanent one-time claims. Follow Aniimo's official channels on X, Discord, YouTube, TikTok, Facebook, and Instagram for another **20 Glimmer each**. This is also your account-security backup: if you ever lose one login method, you have others.
- **Progression milestones** — reaching **Grade I Wayfarer** lets you choose one Rare Prismana, and Aniilog (creature collection) milestones pay out accessories.
- **Login track** — cumulative sign-ins grant a **Random Perfect Aniimo Egg** plus up to seven Adventure Supplies items.
- **Four free outfits** from story progression, Home activities, and cumulative sign-ins.
- **Event currencies** — participating in launch activities pays Sparkling Cubes, Aniipod Ultras, and Tumblers.
- **Mobile pre-registration rewards** — the pre-registration milestones include a mystery Susata egg, the Sunlit Meadow outfit, an avatar frame, capture pods, and premium currency, all granted through the standard reward tracks.

My honest take: the platform-linking Glimmer is the most overlooked item on this list. It takes about three minutes, it is permanent, and doubling your backed-up login methods is worth it even if the currency were nothing. Do it before you get distracted by the actual game — which, to be fair, is a real risk, because the game is good. ## Your First Ten Minutes on Mobile: A Priority Checklist

Servers open at the same instant worldwide, so the first hour is queues. Here is what I would actually do once you are in, in order:

1. **Verify your character is there** before doing anything else. If it is not, stop and check server/sign-in — see the diagnostic above.
2. **Finish any dangling email association** from step one of the account work. The linking rewards stack with it.
3. **Sweep the reward tracks**: platform-linking Glimmer, test reward redemption page (on the website, not in-game), then the event page once you clear level 25.
4. **Claim Boundless Twining** once both logins have registered.
5. **Sync your Homeland** and check your farm — cross-progression means your RV camp and decorations are already there, and early Homelands are the most common thing players neglect for a week. Our [launch day guide's Homeland section](/games/aniimo/launch-day-guide) explains why that is the mistake everyone makes.
6. **Set your graphics target**: 60fps is supported, so if your phone is at or above the recommended column, start there and only step down if thermals complain on long sessions.

And one quality-of-life tip from a week of playing on both screens: set up the mobile client for dailies and the PC/console for sessions. Captures and exploration feel far better on a big screen with a controller, but sign-ins, vendor checks, and event claims are perfect phone-sized tasks. That split is exactly the routine Boundless Twining is designed to reward.
