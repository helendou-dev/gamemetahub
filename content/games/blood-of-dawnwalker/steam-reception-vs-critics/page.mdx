The Blood of Dawnwalker launched on September 3 to a striking split: **83 on Metacritic** from 41 critics, **85 on OpenCritic**, and a sea of praise for its writing and time system — but only **58% positive across 444 Steam user reviews** by end of day one, putting it firmly in "Mixed" territory. The gap between press scores and player reception is one of the widest we have seen on a major single-player RPG this year, and almost all of it traces to one thing: PC performance.

Our [media review roundup](/games/blood-of-dawnwalker/review-roundup-early-verdicts) covered every critic score. This piece focuses on what the Steam review bomb and the [GamesRadar+ report on PC optimization](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/rpg/the-blood-of-dawnwalker-devs-offer-fixes-and-workarounds-as-players-slam-pc-optimization-aaa-price-tag-without-aaa-performance) are actually saying.

## What Players Are Praising

The positive reviews are unusually specific. Players consistently call out three things:

- **Combat that feels like Kingdom Come: Deliverance** — directional parries, weighty encounters, and a learning curve that "gets under your skin" once it clicks. Many reviewers who initially bounced came back and upgraded their recommendation.
- **A genuinely time-aware world** — the 30-day clock is dividing opinion, but the players who love it call it the most stressful and rewarding structure in any 2026 RPG. You cannot do everything, and that forces real choices.
- **Vampire lore that runs deeper than the aesthetic** — the Vrakhiri clan, the night-only traversal, and Coen's dual human/vampire identity are praised as actual mechanical depth, not just a coat of paint.

## What Players Are Slamming

The negative Steam reviews are dominated by a single theme: **PC performance is "trash"** on day one, even for owners of an RTX 5090. The most common complaints:

- **Cutscenes locked to 30 FPS** — a developer-intended choice that has already been worked around by a Nexus Mods "Uncapped Cutscene" patch, and that critics and players alike are asking Rebel Wolves to unlock.
- **UE5 stutter and frame drops in the first village** — reported on systems that exceed the recommended spec, including crashes tied to shader compilation.
- **Missing HDR support**, controller dead-zone drift on diagonal input, choppy camera, character feet not planting correctly, and dialogue that cannot be skipped line-by-line.

Rebel Wolves has publicly responded with a [list of fixes and workarounds](https://tech.sportskeeda.com/gaming-news/all-known-issues-errors-the-blood-dawnwalker): switch to Fullscreen mode to stop stuttering, update your BIOS to prevent shader-build crashes, and drop controller sensitivity from 1.0 to 0.8 to manage diagonal input. They have explicitly said a proper patch is in progress. The $69.99 standard / $79.99 Eclipse edition price tag is the line players keep quoting in frustration — "AAA price tag without AAA performance" is now a recurring phrase in the Steam review thread.

## The Time System Is The Real Cultural Divide

Outside of performance, the most argued-about mechanic is the time cost on learning skills. You have 30 in-game days to save Coen's family, and spending time training is a real trade-off. Critics at PCGamesN, Eurogamer, and VGC called it brilliant; Game Informer and GamesRadar+ called it an "unnecessary tax" that adds friction instead of freedom. Steam reviews split the same way, and the divide is genuinely player-dependent: completionists hate it, decision-RPG fans love it.

## Should You Buy Day One?

If you are on **PC and you care about 60 FPS or HDR**, wait for the first patch — the worst of the issues are clearly on the PC build, and the studio is actively responding. If you are on **PS5 or Xbox Series X**, current reports suggest the console experience is closer to what critics reviewed. If the time system and Kingdom Come-style combat sound like your kind of RPG, the writing, worldbuilding, and dual human/vampire identity are the strongest-reviewed parts of the game. For the deeper story and choice picture, [our preview roundup](/games/blood-of-dawnwalker/preview-roundup-2026s-breakout-rpg) covers what made the early builds feel special. The rest is a question of how much jank you can stomach on launch day.
