## What the Previews Predict Reviewers Will Say

The summer's hands-on previews — roughly four hours each, covering the prologue and a bounded slice of Vale Sangora — skewed strongly positive. Eurogamer floated "2026's breakout RPG," PCGamesN called it their most-wanted game, and Xbox Wire praised "excellent writing and performance." We collected the full quotes in our preview roundup published last week.

The previews also flagged exactly the three fault lines the finished-game reviews will have to cross:

- **Combat over the long haul.** The directional swordplay clicked for some previewers and frustrated others. Divisive in hour two — does it still work in hour forty?
- **The 30-day clock at full scale.** Time-as-currency worked beautifully in the preview slice. Rebel Wolves estimates 50-70 hours for a full playthrough, and no previewer saw the whole structure.
- **Presentation polish.** Facial animation was repeatedly described as "AA, not AAA," and there were early PC performance questions that a day-one patch is expected to address.

<ProTip title="Day-One Performance Watch">
Early launch-week reporting indicates a day-one patch will add a 60fps performance mode on PS5 and Xbox Series X — but not on Xbox Series S, which reportedly stays at 30fps. Performance-mode behavior is exactly the kind of thing day-one reviews will test, so check the console verdicts if you play on Series S.
</ProTip>
