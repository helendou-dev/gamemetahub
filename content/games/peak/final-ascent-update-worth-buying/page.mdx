## What Is PEAK?

PEAK is a co-op climbing roguelite where up to four Scouts work together to scale a procedurally generated mountain. Each run throws different biomes, weather, and hazards at you, and the core loop is simple but brutal: manage your stamina, use items wisely, and physically help (or accidentally kill) your teammates as you scramble toward the summit. It launched in June 2025 to overwhelming acclaim — 135,000+ Steam reviews at ~95% positive — and the developers have been clear from day one that it was never meant to be a live-service game.

## Ascent 8 and the Reworked Ascent 7

PEAK's difficulty system uses Ascent tiers — optional modifiers that stack extra challenges onto a run. The update adds **Ascent 8**, the final tier, but the developers have deliberately kept its contents secret. Their reasoning: describing it would be a spoiler.

Ascent 7 has also been reworked. Previously, it blocked revives entirely — a design the developers admitted made the game less fun and was meaningless in solo play. Now it starts you cursed, and each revive adds more **Curse**, a negative status that permanently lowers your maximum stamina and does not wear off on its own. The punishment is still real, but it no longer locks players out of the game entirely.

<ProTip title="Curse Is Permanent">
Unlike most status effects in PEAK, Curse does not decay over time. Every death on Ascent 7+ makes the rest of the run harder, so prioritize survival over speed.
</ProTip>

## New Items: Jetpack, Glider, Warp Fungus, and Clown Luggage

The update adds several new tools that change how you approach the climb:

- **Jetpack** — straightforward vertical boost for tricky ascents.
- **Glider** — works like the one in Breath of the Wild, letting you cover horizontal distance after a jump.
- **Warp Fungus** — throw it to see what happens (the devs are being coy, but it teleports you).
- **Clown Luggage** — a new luggage type that dispenses wacky items like lollipops, cannons, and balloon bunches. These items no longer appear in regular climber luggage, making Clown Luggage the only way to get them.

Scoutmaster Myres himself speaks for the first time in this update — voiced by Xalavier Nelson Jr. (founder of Strange Scaffold and creator of El Paso, Elsewhere). The developers have also teased undisclosed "SUPER SECRET" content reserved for what they call "the most determined scouts," which connects to Ascent 8 and the island's hidden story.

## Beginner Tips for New Players

If you're buying PEAK during the sale, here's how to get started efficiently:

1. **Play with friends if possible** — the chaos of four-player co-op is the game's core appeal, and voice communication makes every climb dramatically easier.
2. **Learn the biomes before pushing Ascent tiers** — each area has distinct hazard patterns. Spend your first few runs on base difficulty memorizing them.
3. **Don't hoard items** — PEAK's runs are short. A jetpack or glider used now is better than one saved for a summit you might not reach.
4. **Stay close to teammates** — the game rewards physical cooperation. You can boost each other up ledges and catch falling scouts, but only if you're nearby.
5. **Try Clown Luggage early** — the wacky items it dispenses are powerful in ways regular gear isn't, and they're exclusive to this luggage type.

## FAQ

### Is The Final Ascent a free update?

Yes. The Final Ascent is a free download for all existing PEAK owners. You do not need to buy anything extra.

### Will PEAK get more content updates after this?

No. The developers have confirmed The Final Ascent is the last major content update. Future patches will focus on bug fixes and stability. Console ports with cross-platform play are planned for later in 2026.

### Is PEAK on PlayStation, Xbox, or Switch?

Not yet. PEAK is currently PC-only (Steam). Console versions are in development with cross-platform play planned, but no release date has been announced.

### Can I play PEAK solo?

Yes, but it's designed for four-player co-op. Solo play is possible and the Ascent 7 rework specifically addresses solo viability, but the game is at its best with friends.

### What is Ascent 8?

Ascent 8 is the new final difficulty tier. Its contents are intentionally kept secret by the developers — they describe it as something where "the description itself is a spoiler." You'll need to reach it to find out.
