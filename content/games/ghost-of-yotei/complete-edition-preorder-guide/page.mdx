Sucker Punch is treating Ghost of Yōtei's first anniversary as a full relaunch. The **Ghost of Yōtei Complete Edition** lands on **October 1, 2026**, bundling the base game, both new content drops, and every prior Digital Deluxe item. The catch: if you want Jin Sakai's family armor as an early unlock, you need to pre-order by **September 30, 2026** — just four days away.

Here's what the package actually includes, how the two purchase paths compare, and why even players who skip the upgrade still get something on October 1.

## What's Inside the Complete Edition

The $69.99 Complete Edition is aimed at new players. It contains:

- The full Ghost of Yōtei base campaign
- Ghost of Yōtei: Legends, the four-player online co-op mode
- All Digital Deluxe Edition content plus the Charm of Hokkyokusei
- **Echoes of Sekigahara**, a narrative expansion split between the Battle of Sekigahara and a present-day valley in Ezo
- **Most Wanted**, a single-player roguelike survival mode with bounty runs, perk builds, and multiple playable characters
- New photo mode stamps, NG+ cosmetics, and Trophies for both expansions

If you already own the game, the **$14.99 Complete Edition Upgrade** unlocks Echoes of Sekigahara and Most Wanted without making you rebuy the campaign.

The upgrade is the better value for existing owners. At $14.99, it undercuts Ghost of Tsushima's Iki Island expansion by $5 and delivers both a story DLC and a replayable roguelike mode.

## Sakai Armor Early Unlock — The Real Deadline

Pre-ordering either the Complete Edition or the upgrade by **September 30** grants an **early unlock of the Sakai Armor** on October 1. Normally, the armor is tied to progress in Most Wanted; the pre-order bonus lets Atsu wear Jin Sakai's iconic family set immediately.

This is purely cosmetic and nostalgic, but for players who came to Yōtei from Ghost of Tsushima, it's the closest thing to a cross-game tribute. After September 30, the $14.99 upgrade price stays the same — only the armor unlock disappears.

## Free Patch 2.0 for All Owners

Even if you buy nothing, Sucker Punch is pushing **Patch v.2.0** to all Ghost of Yōtei owners on October 1. The free update adds:

- **Beauty of Yōtei mode**: a screensaver-style way to explore Ezo in Photo Mode
- Armor transmog: keep the look of one set while using another's stats
- New Watanabe Mode music tracks
- A high-contrast accessibility option
- Additional photo mode stamps

For anyone on the fence, this free patch is a quiet signal that the game is still getting quality-of-life support a full year out.

## The Bottom Line

The Complete Edition is the cleanest entry point for new players, while the $14.99 upgrade is a no-brainer for existing owners who want more Yōtei. Just don't sleep on the September 30 deadline if the Sakai Armor matters to you.
