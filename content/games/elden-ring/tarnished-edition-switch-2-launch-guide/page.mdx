Elden Ring has sold over 28 million copies across PS4, PS5, Xbox, and PC. On August 28, 2026, it finally lands on Nintendo Switch 2 as the **Tarnished Edition** — a complete package bundling the base game, the Shadow of the Erdtree expansion, and a set of new content designed for newcomers to FromSoftware's punishing world.

The journey to this release was not smooth. Early demos at Gamescom 2025 reportedly ran at around 15 FPS, prompting a delay into 2026. Bandai Namco and FromSoftware used the extra months to resolve an ARM CPU bottleneck, and recent preview builds from Famitsu and Denfaminicogamer paint a far healthier picture. The question now is whether 30 FPS is enough for the Lands Between.

## Performance: 30 FPS Target, No 60 FPS Mode

This is the section that matters most. Here is what we know from preview coverage as of August 22, 2026:

### Docked Mode

Docked mode targets a **stable 30 FPS**. Recent preview builds from Famitsu and Denfaminicogamer show notable improvements over the rough 2025 demo. Open-world traversal through Limgrave is smoother, with reduced frame stuttering when entering dense areas. Boss footage lacks the repeated hitches that plagued the early build.

Denfaminicogamer's hands-on tester, who had logged over 100 hours on PC, reported "no frame drops or stutters" in either TV or handheld mode during a two-and-a-half-hour session with a development build. Controls via the Switch 2 Pro Controller responded immediately.

### Handheld Mode

Handheld mode maintains visual fidelity surprisingly close to docked play — similar lighting, environmental detail, and overall presentation. Clear-weather exploration through Limgrave looks stable.

However, demanding boss encounters with heavy particle effects can cause frame rates to dip **below 30 FPS** in handheld mode. The CPU and GPU operate at lower power limits when undocked, which puts more pressure on the system during combat-heavy scenes.

### No 60 FPS Option

There is no confirmed 60 FPS mode on Switch 2. The PS5 and Xbox Series X versions offer a 60 FPS performance mode; the Switch 2 edition does not. If you want high-refresh combat, stick to those platforms or a capable PC.

## Game-Key Card Format: What It Means

The physical edition of Tarnished Edition uses Nintendo's **Game-Key Card** format. This is important to understand before you buy:

- The cartridge contains **no game data** — it is essentially a physical license key
- You must download approximately **41 GB** of game data from the eShop
- An internet connection is required for the initial download
- The cartridge must be inserted to play (it is not a one-time activation)

If you were planning to buy physical to save storage space, this changes the equation. The Game-Key Card is primarily about resale value — you can lend or sell the cartridge to another Switch 2 owner. But you still need the same 41 GB of storage either way.

## Should You Buy It?

### Buy the Switch 2 edition if:

- You want to play Elden Ring portably and do not own it on any other platform
- You are okay with 30 FPS combat (the original 2022 release ran at 30 FPS on PS4 and Xbox One)

## Controls: Pro Controller Recommended

Preview coverage confirms that the Switch 2 Pro Controller provides the best experience. Denfaminicogamer's tester noted that controls felt natural and responsive — veterans from PC or console can jump in without learning an entirely new control scheme.

For handheld mode, the built-in Joy-Con 2 controllers work fine for exploration and standard combat. For precision-heavy boss encounters, the Pro Controller's larger grips and more defined D-pad are worth the investment.

## FAQ

### Does Tarnished Edition include Shadow of the Erdtree?

Yes. The Switch 2 edition bundles the complete base game and the full Shadow of the Erdtree expansion in one package.

### Can I play online with friends on other platforms?

No. The Switch 2 version does not support cross-platform multiplayer. Online play is limited to other Switch 2 players — three-player co-op, PvP invasions, and arena duels.

### Is the 30 FPS target playable for Elden Ring?

Yes. The original 2022 release ran at 30 FPS on PS4 and Xbox One, and millions of players completed the game at that frame rate. The key factor is frame pacing consistency — a stable 30 FPS is far more playable than a volatile frame rate that occasionally hits 60 but stutters during combat.

### Will the final build perform better than the previews?

Possibly. Preview builds are not final code. However, Bandai Namco has not published official frame-rate or resolution targets, so treat all performance claims as pre-release until August 28 launch-day reports arrive.

### What languages are supported?

Korean and Chinese are among the confirmed supported languages, alongside English, Japanese, French, German, Spanish, Italian, and others.
