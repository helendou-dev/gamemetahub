## What Is Black Myth: Zhong Kui?

**Black Myth: Zhong Kui** (黑神话：钟馗) is the second title in Game Science's Black Myth series, officially revealed during the **[Gamescom 2025 Opening Night Live](https://www.gamescom.global/en)** showcase on August 20, 2026. The game is a single-player action RPG centered on **Zhong Kui**, the legendary ghost-hunting deity from Chinese folklore.

Following the unprecedented success of Black Myth: Wukong — which sold over **30 million copies** globally — Game Science is expanding the Black Myth universe with a new protagonist, new story, and new combat mechanics. CEO **Feng Ji** confirmed this is not a direct sequel or DLC, but an entirely new entry designed to push the series forward.

> "DLC would of course be a fine choice. But right now, we'd rather make a new Black Myth game — a new hero, new gameplay, new visuals, new technology, a new story." — Feng Ji, Game Science CEO

---

## The Gamescom 2025 Reveal

The announcement came as the **closing act** of Gamescom Opening Night Live — the first time a Chinese-developed game has received the prestigious final slot at the world's largest gaming convention. Host **Geoff Keighley** introduced Zhong Kui as "the god who walks between hell and the mortal world, hunting ghosts."

The reveal trailer, a roughly **2-minute CGI cinematic**, showed:

- **Zhong Kui riding a massive tiger** through a dark, supernatural landscape
- **Sword combat against hordes of demons** in sprawling underworld environments
- **Ink-wash painting aesthetics** blended with Unreal Engine 5's cinematic realism
- **Traditional Chinese architecture** — palace halls, crimson gates, spirit lanterns

Within hours, the trailer surpassed **10 million views** on Bilibili and trended at #1 across Chinese social media. Internationally, Wikipedia reported a **300% surge** in searches for the "Zhong Kui" entry, as fans worldwide raced to learn about the folklore behind the new protagonist.

## Who Is Zhong Kui? The Mythology

| Aspect | Detail |
|:--|:--|
| **Origin** | Tang Dynasty (7th century) Chinese folklore |
| **Title** | Ghost Catcher / Demon Slayer (捉鬼天师) |
| **Appearance** | Fierce warrior with a dark beard, often depicted wielding a sword |
| **Domain** | Walks between the mortal world and the underworld |
| **Companions** | A tiger mount; sometimes accompanied by demon subordinates |
| **Cultural Status** | One of China's most recognizable mythological figures |

Zhong Kui is fundamentally different from Sun Wukong. While Wukong is chaotic, rebellious, and trickster-coded, Zhong Kui is a **force of order** — a divine enforcer who hunts malevolent spirits and maintains the boundary between worlds. This tonal shift suggests Black Myth: Zhong Kui will offer a **darker, more gothic atmosphere** compared to Wukong's vibrant mythological adventure.

## Story & Setting

Details on the narrative are limited, but Game Science has confirmed the game draws from **Chinese ghost lore and folk horror traditions**. Art Director **Yang Qi** revealed the project's origin:

> "The inspiration came from a dream — I dreamt of Zhong Kui riding a tiger. After discussion, we decided this was the story we needed to tell."

The game is expected to explore the **boundary between the living world and the underworld**, with Zhong Kui traveling between realms to hunt rogue spirits. Unlike Wukong's journey to the West, Zhong Kui's story is rooted in **exorcism, judgment, and cosmic balance** — drawing from classic Chinese texts like _Strange Tales from a Chinese Studio_ (聊斋志异).

## FAQ

### Is Black Myth: Zhong Kui a sequel to Wukong?

No. It is a **standalone new entry** in the Black Myth series, set in a different mythological world with a new protagonist. Feng Ji explicitly described it as "not a direct sequel — a new hero, a new story."

### When will Black Myth: Zhong Kui release?

No release date has been announced. Based on Wukong's ~5-year development cycle, the **earliest estimate is 2029-2030**. The game is in early development.

### Will Zhong Kui have the same combat as Wukong?

It will share the **high-difficulty, transformation-based combat skeleton** but introduce significant new mechanics. Feng Ji promised the game will "refresh the player experience" rather than rehashing Wukong's systems.

### What does "Zhong Kui" mean?

Zhong Kui (钟馗) is a legendary figure in Chinese folklore — a **ghost-hunting deity** who patrols the boundary between the living world and the underworld. He is traditionally depicted as a fierce warrior with a sword and a tiger mount.

### Why did Game Science choose Zhong Kui instead of making Wukong DLC?

Feng Ji explained that player expectations for Wukong DLC had become so high — with fan concepts outpacing the team's own plans — that creating something **genuinely surprising** under those constraints was risky. Starting fresh with a new hero allows the team to innovate freely without being boxed in by Wukong's established world and mechanics.

### Is there gameplay footage available?

**No.** The Gamescom 2026 reveal was a CGI cinematic trailer. Art Director Yang Qi confirmed the game has "no playable content worth showing yet." Expect gameplay reveals in future annual updates.
