Jagex's open-world RuneScape survival craft is leaving Early Access on **September 15, 2026** with version 1.0 — and unlike most survival games that fizzle out of Early Access, this one is shipping a full raid, a new gear tier, a combat rework, and Jagex's first ever same-day multi-platform launch in one drop. The headline is Kuldra, the five-headed God-Eater dragon who has been the implied final boss since the April 2025 EA launch. The headline-grabber is that you can fight her on PC, PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, and Nintendo Switch 2 at exactly the same time, with crossplay across all four.

RuneScape: Dragonwilds was a quiet 600K-units-in-a-week hit on launch and has spent the last 17 months quietly growing into something closer to a Valheim-clone-that-actually-has-RuneScape's-skill-system, which is the part EA reviewers could not have scored. If you bounced off it during Early Access, **1.0 is the moment to look again** — and if you are a long-time RuneScape player who never tried Dragonwilds because "it looked survival-icky," the Runite gear and Kuldra raid are the same flavor of dragon grinding you already love.

## What Actually Lands on September 15

Jagex walked through the full 1.0 lineup in [the official launch announcement](https://www.doomhosting.com/blog/runescape-dragonwilds-1-0-release-september-15-2026) and the long-term [Dragonwilds roadmap wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Uu):

- **Scorned Wilderness raid** — the first proper raid in Dragonwilds, structured around floating islands between which players fight toward Kuldra. Solo or co-op. Most mechanics are deliberately kept secret until launch.
- **Kuldra, the God-Eater Queen** — five-headed Black Dragon who ties off the entire EA story arc. Each head channels a different Gielinor dragon's powers, and confirmed drops include a Dragon Scimitar, a Dragon Crossbow, and a victory cape. The full Rare Drop Table is being held back.
- **Runite tier** — adds Rune Armour, Black Dragonhide gear, and Ancestral Robes (the magic successor to Infinity Robes).
- **All 13 skills releasable to 99** — Agility, added in July 2026, completes the lineup.
- **Combat rework** — faster blocking, animation cancelling for spells and bows, sprint attacks, parries that refund 50% Stamina and unlock a cinematic Visceral Attack counter, plus Wands and the classic RuneScape Special Attack bar.

## Why the Multi-Platform Push Matters

Most survival crafting games treat console as a port afterthought. Jagex is launching **all four platforms simultaneously** on September 15, and the dedicated servers that shipped with the Dowdun Reach update in March are now crossplay-capable. A single Pterodactyl-hosted world can hold your Switch 2 friend, your PS5 friend, and your Steam friend at the same time. That is a quiet but huge reason pre-launch numbers jumped: Jagex hit **52,641 concurrent players** on Steam alone back in April 2025, and adding three console audiences at once with crossplay could push that meaningfully higher at launch.

Subscription deals are also part of the marketing calculus. Dragonwilds is set to land on **Xbox Game Pass day one** and **PS Plus Extra day one**, the same week as the paid release. Anyone holding a subs-only wait-and-see is one toggle away from 1.0.

## What to Do Before You Log In

If you already have a Dowdun Reach-era save, take a manual backup the night before — major version transitions historically break a small percentage of saves across this genre, and there is no public test branch for 1.0, so the build you have on September 15 is the build everyone has. If you are renting a server, plan one RAM tier up for launch week; population spikes consistently run 3x–5x normal for the first fourteen days on this kind of release.

If you are starting fresh on September 15, the rational priority order for the first session is now: get Woodcutting, Mining, and Construction to ~50 while grabbing the **Magic Carpet** from Umbral Sands, because the Runite-tier grind in Scorned Wilderness assumes you already have fast travel and a base. Fishing and Cooking come next, because the new stamina economy punishes raw food more than the EA version did. Prayer is not on the 1.0 patch — it is the December "Luminance" winter update — so do not waste the first week chasing it.

For context on what a major survival game launch window actually looks like in practice — version bumps, the broken-mods phase, the crossplay math — Valheim 1.0 followed the same playbook ten days earlier, and [our Valheim 1.0 launch guide](/games/valheim/1-0-launch-guide) walks through the bumps we hit. Dragonwilds will almost certainly repeat at least two of them.

## What to Watch

The big unknown is server stability when a Switch 2 client and a Steam client try to join the same world for the first time. Jagex has had nine months of dedicated server hardening since Dowdun Reach, but no real way to load-test console clients until the embargo drops. September 15 is the moment the live network gets its dress rehearsal. Watch the official [Dragonwilds Discord](https://discord.com/invite/dragonwilds) for the first 72 hours — that is where the "version mismatch" fixes will land first.
