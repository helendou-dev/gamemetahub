RuneScape: Dragonwilds 1.0 lands **tomorrow, September 15, 2026** on PC, PS5, Xbox Series X|S, and Switch 2 with the Scorned Wilderness raid as its crown jewel — the five-headed dragon queen Kuldra, fought across floating islands torn apart by wild anima. We already covered [what 1.0 ships](/games/runescape-dragonwilds/1-0-launch-guide). This is the sharper question: what does your character actually need *before* you set foot in the raid? Because the answer is not "log in and walk in" — a fresh 1.0 character cannot enter the zone at all.

## The Gate: Icthlarin's Little Helper

Entry to the Scorned Wilderness is gated behind **Icthlarin's Little Helper**, the game's eighth primary quest and the fourth chapter of the main storyline. It is a long one, and it is not optional. The chain, condensed:

- Complete **Black Knight's Fortress**, then defeat the **Black Knight Titan** and interact with the Door to the Umbral Sands at The Bastion.
- In Umbral Sands, earn trust with **three Moon Garou clans** — Alcarrid, Manafem, and Dunes of Uzzer — by clearing their **nine faction tasks** (three per camp, 200 Garou Chits each).
- Collect the three **mirror lenses**, then survive the **ten-round Fight Cave** — round 10 drops Tok-Xil, who constantly spawns trash mobs until he hits 50% health.
- Immediately after round 10, fight the desert tyrant **Fuzan**. The catch: teleporting out to restock resets the entire gauntlet.

Bring logistics, not just a sword — Mithril pickaxe or better, an Adamant logging axe, a spade, 3 adamant bars, 1 clean water, and 3 each of raw farm meat, kalphite shell fragments, and cactus steaks. Craft **two or more Lodestones** before you enter: the desert has a single permanent regional one, and the Fight Cave entrance deserves a second. Enter the desert **at night, fully rested** — sun exposure inflicts Scorch, and opening hours matter.

The quest also hands you the **Magic Carpet** partway through the Alcarrid camp reputation grind. That mount is the region's traversal answer — faster over dirt, higher jumps, less fall damage — and you will want it long before the raid.

## The Stats: All 18 Sanguine Urns

Since the health rework, Dragonwilds has no shield bar and no healing potions. Damage lands directly on your HP pool, and the only permanent way to grow that pool is exploration: **Sanguine Urns**, scattered across Ashenfall, each granting **+10 maximum health, permanently**.

- **18 urns exist.** Base health is 100; collecting every urn puts you at **280 HP**.
- The boosts **persist through death and across worlds, including dedicated servers** — nothing to re-earn on a wipe.
- Urns show an icon on the world map and compass within roughly 50–60 meters; several hide in **caves** (the Whispering Swamp one sits in the Gauru Druid cave, east entrance by the waterfall). If the compass points at an unreachable spot, look for a cave mouth.

If you do one thing before the raid tomorrow, sweep the urn map. 180 extra HP is the difference between a dragonfire hit you shrug off and a corpse run.

## The Loadout: Food, Not Potions

The rework removed healing potions but kept stat potions, which flips your inventory logic:

- **Cooked food heals instantly** — carry it into the fight, and keep eating even at full health, because health regeneration scales with how well fed you are (every 25 Sustenance adds +0.5 HP/sec, up to 2.0/sec). **Fish are best-in-class healing**, so Fishing is now a survivability skill.
- **A successful parry refunds 50% of your maximum stamina** — blocking is survivability by another route, and the 1.0 combat rework adds animation cancelling, sprint attacks, and Visceral Attack counters.
- For Fuzan, a **Super Antifire** blunts fire burn — but drinking it wipes your active damage potions, so time it.
- Kuldra is a Black Dragon; assume antifire discipline applies to her five heads too.

On gear level: Fight Cave veterans recommend a **minimum Gear Power Level of 7** before attempting the gauntlet that ends in Fuzan, and melee players should pack a magic staff for the ranged waves.

## The Caveat: Ignore Every Dragon Stat Table

One warning worth its own section: the item *names* for Scorned Wilderness loot are confirmed — nineteen Dragon pieces across armour, one- and two-handed melee, a crossbow, arrows and bolts, alongside rune equipment, Ancestral Robes, and black dragonhide — but **every stat number attached to Dragon gear before September 15 is fake**. The table currently circulating is Adamant's numbers copied onto placeholder rows. Treat any pre-launch "Dragon Scimitar: 76–160" list as scaffolding, not data.

## Before You Log In

Back up your save manually tonight — there is no public test branch for 1.0, so the build everyone gets tomorrow is untested at scale. Server renters should plan a RAM tier up for launch week. And if you're playing cross-platform with a Switch 2 or PS5 friend, watch the official Dragonwilds Discord for the first 72 hours — version-mismatch fixes land there first.

The raid will still be there Thursday. Your health pool is the only thing you can permanently upgrade before then.
