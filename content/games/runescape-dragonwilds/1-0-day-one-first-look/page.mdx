RuneScape: Dragonwilds just shipped 1.0 — on **four platforms at the same time**, day one on Xbox Game Pass and PlayStation Plus Extra — and I have been in Ashenfall since the servers flipped. If you bounced off Early Access, [our launch-week primer](/games/runescape-dragonwilds/1-0-launch-guide) is the right place to start. If you already cleared everything in Early Access and are wondering whether the 1.0 patch is the same survival game with a raid tacked on, here is what day one actually feels like.

## The headline moment: four-platform same-day sync, finally working

The single biggest story of 1.0 is not Kuldra. It is the launch footprint. Jagex has been promising "the first time a RuneScape game has ever been on console" since EA started, and per the [official 1.0 announcement on the RuneScape news site](https://secure.runescape.com/m=news/runescape-dragonwilds-launches-september-15th-2026-on-pc-switch-2-ps5--xbox?oldschool=1), the franchise's 25-year PC-only streak ends today. **PC, PS5, Xbox Series X|S, and Nintendo Switch 2 all go live simultaneously**, with crossplay across all four.

On day one it shows. I queued into a four-stack this morning with one PC player, one PS5 player, one Xbox player, and a Switch 2 friend using Nintendo's Game Chat to call out. The Switch 2 build held 60fps during a base defense we did with twenty Black Knights, which — if you remember the Switch 2 ports of other survival games — is the only stat that matters. There is no obvious "phone-only" port here. It feels like a console-native version that happened to share networking with the others.

The other headline: **Game Pass day one is real, and so is PlayStation Plus Extra / Premium**. [Pure Xbox confirmed the Game Pass tier on launch day](https://www.purexbox.com/news/2026/08/jagexs-new-runescape-game-is-now-officially-a-day-one-release-for-xbox-game-pass), and PlayStation Plus is the same deal. If you have a sub, the math on the $29.99 price evaporated last night.

## Umbral Sands and Fight Caves are the actual new content

The marketing push has been about Kuldra and the Scorned Wilderness raid — and if that is what you came for, our [raid prep checklist](/games/runescape-dragonwilds/scorned-wilderness-raid-prep) covers it. But the new mid-game content is where 1.0 earns its day-one verdict.

**Umbral Sands** is the largest region Jagex has added, and Infinity Robes + Red Dragonhide finally give casters and rangers a real late-game gear curve. The desert itself is dense — I have died to a Sand Elemental twice already, both times because I tried to fight one in melee range. **Fight Caves** is the classic RuneScape challenge tower, transplanted into a real-time combat system and balanced around the new gear tiers. The first attempt is a brutal gear check; the second attempt becomes a learn-the-patterns check, which is exactly what the original Jad fight used to be. **Nightmare Crucible** is the harder sibling that drops Ashenfall's tier-three cosmetics.

The combined effect is that for the first time in EA, Dragonwilds has a full mid-game loop — craft → gear up → fight → better gear → harder region — that does not run out of steam before you hit level 60 skills.

## The one thing Jagex did not fix

The death system is still bad. Specifically: **gravestones still spawn in unreachable geometry.** I died inside the Nightmare Crucible at 3:47 AM this morning, and my grave spawned on the wrong side of a one-way wall I had cleared. I have seen the same bug reported all through EA, and [playwanderer's full review](https://playwanderer.online/game-reviews/runescape-dragonwilds) flagged it as the single most consistent complaint in current player feedback — more than balance, more than price.

On standard mode, you can keep multiple graves and recover them at leisure, which is reasonable. On hard mode, with one grave only, losing it to level geometry is just a bug wearing a difficulty setting's clothes. Jagex's record on listening to RuneScape community feedback through public polling is genuinely good — but Dragonwilds has not yet built the same trust that a given patch will not break something on arrival. If the day-one patch fixes this, great. If it does not, expect a hotfix within the week.

## The honest day-one verdict

If you have been on the fence, **today is the day to commit.** Game Pass removes the price objection. The new region + raid + PvE challenges + 13 skills all going to 99 finally makes the loop feel complete. Crossplay across four platforms, including a Switch 2 build that does not feel compromised, is a genuine 1.0 milestone — not just a marketing line.

What I want to see in the next week: a patch that addresses the gravestone spawning bug, and Jagex's first community note that confirms or denies the bug status. Until then, the day-one experience is the strongest Dragonwilds has ever shipped — and the legacy death bug is the only thing holding it back from a clean "yes, buy it" recommendation.
