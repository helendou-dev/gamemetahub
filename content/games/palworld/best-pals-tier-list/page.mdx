## How These Tiers Work

Tier placement is based on my testing across four criteria:

1. **Raw performance** — damage output, work speed, or traversal speed compared to alternatives
2. **Versatility** — can this Pal handle multiple roles, or is it a one-trick?
3. **Accessibility** — how hard is it to obtain and breed? A Pal that requires endgame materials ranks lower than one you can get mid-game
4. **1.0 synergy** — does this Pal benefit from the new Awakening system, Work Suitability Level 10, or Mutation breeding?

I've cross-referenced my findings with community data from [Game8's combat tier list](https://game8.co/games/Palworld/archives/440279) and [SlashSkill's comprehensive rankings](https://slashskill.com/palworld-tier-list-best-pals-for-combat-base-work-and-mounts-in-1-0/) to make sure I'm not missing anything. ## S-Tier Combat Pals — The Ones You Build Around

### Knocklem Ignis

Knocklem was already strong in Early Access, and the Ignis variant from 1.0's Sunreach region pushed it into a league of its own. The fire-elemental typing combined with its massive base attack stat means it melts Tower Bosses faster than anything else I've tested. I took down the Sunreach Tower Boss in under 3 minutes with a fully condensed Knocklem Ignis — that's a minute and a half faster than my previous best with a Frostallion team.

The catch? Knocklem Ignis only spawns in the volcanic zones of Sunreach, and it's rare. You'll need to be Level 55+ to survive the area, and breeding for good passives takes time. But if you're building an endgame combat team, this is your starting point.

### Bellanoir Libero

Bellanoir was the first raid boss Pal added to Palworld, and the Libero variant introduced in 1.0 is arguably the strongest dark-elemental damage dealer in the game. Its Partner Skill increases damage as its HP decreases, which creates a high-risk high-reward playstyle that I genuinely enjoy. You're managing positioning to keep Bellanoir Libero at low HP without it dying — it's the most engaging combat Pal to pilot.

I got destroyed by the World Tree boss three times before I figured out that Bellanoir Libero's skill synergizes with the new "World Tree" passive skills that trade defense for attack. Stack those passives, keep HP at 30%, and watch health bars evaporate. Not for the faint of heart, but nothing else matches its ceiling.

### Jormuntide Ignis

Jormuntide Ignis is the most reliable S-tier pick. Where Bellanoir Libero is a glass cannon and Knocklem Ignis is hard to obtain, Jormuntide Ignis is straightforward: high HP, high attack, fire typing that covers the majority of early-to-mid game content, and a Partner Skill that boosts fire damage across your team.

I recommend Jormuntide Ignis to every player who asks "what should I breed first?" It's obtainable through breeding relatively early, its base stats are forgiving, and it remains viable all the way through World Tree content. Skip nothing, fear nothing — just breed a Jormuntide Ignis.

### Frostallion

Frostallion remains the best ice-elemental combat Pal, and ice is critical for the volcanic zones and several Tower Bosses. Its Partner Skill freezes enemies on hit, which is one of the few crowd control effects that works on boss-type enemies. I keep one in my team at all times specifically for Tower Bosses that resist fire.

Frostallion is also the fastest flying mount in the game before you unlock Jetragon, which means it doubles as your traversal Pal until endgame. If you can only breed two S-tier Pals, make them Jormuntide Ignis and Frostallion — that dual coverage handles 80% of the game's content.

### Eidrolon Ignis

Eidrolon Ignis is the new kid on the block — a 1.0-exclusive Pal from the World Tree region. Its lightning typing is essential for the aquatic enemies in the World Tree's lower levels, and its Partner Skill chains damage between multiple targets. In wave-based raid defense, nothing clears faster.

The problem is accessibility. You need to reach the World Tree, which is endgame content, and Eidrolon Ignis spawns are rare. By the time you can reliably hunt one, you may already have a built team. I'd call it a luxury pick — incredible if you get it, but not worth stalling your progression to chase.

## A-Tier Combat Pals — Reliable and Accessible

### Anubis

Anubis has been a community favorite since Early Access, and 1.0 didn't change that. Its ground typing covers electric enemies, its Partner Skill boosts attack power, and it's breedable from mid-game. If you can't get Knocklem Ignis yet, Anubis is your combat workhorse.

### Shaolong

Shaolong is the dragon-elemental Pal that 1.0 players are sleeping on. Dragon typing is neutral against most enemies, which sounds like a weakness but is actually a strength — you always have relevant damage. Its Partner Skill boosts dragon-type damage, and with the new Work Suitability Level 10 system, Shaoling also functions as a top-tier handiwork Pal for your base.

### Dandilord

Dandilord's grass typing makes it essential for the desert zones and several Sunreach encounters. Its Partner Skill boosts grass damage and has a healing component that extends survivability in long fights. I run Dandilord specifically for the desert Tower Boss, where fire and electric Pals get resisted.

### Neptilius

Neptilius is the new water-elemental legend from 1.0, and it's also the fastest water mount in the game. In combat, its Partner Skill creates a water shield that absorbs damage — the only Pal with a built-in defensive cooldown. This makes Neptilius the best "tank" Pal for players who want survivability over raw DPS. Pair it with a glass cannon like Bellanoir Libero and you have a balanced duo.

### Hartalis

Hartalis is the fastest ground mount in 1.0 and a solid combat Pal. Its Partner Skill boosts movement speed and attack simultaneously, making it the best "engage" Pal — you ride in, dismount, and immediately have a speed-buffed combat Pal ready to fight. For open-world encounters and raid defense where positioning matters, Hartalis is unmatched.

## 1.0 Systems That Changed the Meta

### Work Suitability Level 10

The Work Suitability system expanding from 5 to 10 levels means the gap between a "good" base Pal and a "great" one is now twice as wide. A Pal that maxes at Level 5 suitability is producing roughly 50% less output than one that reaches Level 10. This makes job specialization mandatory — your old generalist base team from Early Access is significantly less efficient.

### Awakening System

The Awakening system lets you push Pals past their natural stat caps using Radiant Gems from the World Tree. This doesn't change tier placements directly — an S-tier Pal with Awakening is still better than an A-tier Pal with Awakening — but it does make investing in your core team more rewarding. Don't spread Radiant Gems across ten Pals; pour them into your top 3-4 combat Pals and your best base workers.

### Mutation Breeding

Mutation breeding introduces a chance for eggs to hatch with enhanced stats and unique passive skills. This is the real endgame for min-maxers. A mutated Jormuntide Ignis with the right passive skills will out-damage a non-mutated one by 30-40%. Stock up on Deluxe Vegetable Cakes before you start serious breeding — the mutation rate is low enough that you'll go through dozens of eggs.

<ProTip title="Don't Sleep on Condensation">

With 1.0 reducing max Condensation from 116 copies to 48, fully condensing a Pal is 60% less grind than before. This is the single biggest QoL improvement for endgame building. Prioritize condensing your combat Pals first — the stat boost from 4-star condensation is larger than most passive skills provide.

</ProTip>

## Team Composition Recommendations

Here's what I'd build for different stages of the game:

### Early-Game Team (Levels 1-40)

- **Combat:** Jormuntide Ignis + Anubis + Frostallion
- **Mount:** Rayhound (ground) + Azumarin (water)
- **Base:** Lyleen (planting) + Astegon (mining) + Orserk (generator)

This team is entirely breedable before reaching Sunreach. Jormuntide Ignis covers fire damage, Anubis handles ground, and Frostallion gives you ice coverage plus a flying mount. For your base, these three cover the essential production loop.

### Endgame Team (Levels 55-80)

- **Combat:** Knocklem Ignis + Bellanoir Libero + Neptilius + Eidrolon Ignis
- **Mount:** Jetragon (flying) + Hartalis (ground) + Neptilius (water, doubles as combat)
- **Base:** Selyne (handiwork) + Astegon (mining) + Helzephyr + Knocklem (transport duo) + Frostallion Noct (cooling)

This is the team I'm currently running. Neptilius pulls double duty as combat tank and water mount, which frees up a team slot. The transport duo of Helzephyr and Knocklem eliminates all base production stalls. If you're pushing World Tree content, this is the goal.

## Pals I Tried and Dropped

Not every popular Pal earned a spot on this list. Here are a few I tested extensively but can't recommend:

- **Grizzbolt:** The poster child of Palworld is a B-tier combat Pal at best. Its electric typing is outclassed by Eidrolon Ignis, and its Partner Skill doesn't scale well into endgame. I wanted to love it because the design is iconic, but the numbers don't lie.
- **Orserk (combat):** Orserk is S-tier for base generator work, but in combat, its slow attack animations get it killed against Tower Bosses. Don't try to make it a dual-purpose Pal.
- **Suzaku Aqua:** Decent water combat Pal, but Neptilius completely outclasses it in 1.0. If you already invested in Suzaku Aqua, it's fine for mid-game — just don't pour more resources into it.

## FAQ

### What is the best Pal in Palworld 1.0?

For combat, Knocklem Ignis is the strongest single-target damage dealer. For base work, Astegon is the best mining Pal and Selyne is the best handiwork Pal. For mounts, Jetragon is the fastest flyer. There's no single "best" Pal — it depends on what you need.

### How many Pals are in Palworld 1.0?

287 total Pals — 215 from Early Access plus 72 new in 1.0 (47 original species and 25 variants). The new Pals are found primarily in Sunreach and the World Tree regions.

### Should I condense or Awakening first?

Condense first. Condensation provides a guaranteed stat boost with predictable resource requirements. Awakening is RNG-dependent (Radiant Gem drops) and should be layered on top of an already-condensed Pal. For a full breakdown of 1.0 systems, check our [Palworld 1.0 Complete Guide](/games/palworld/complete-guide-1-0).

### What's the fastest mount in Palworld 1.0?

Jetragon is the fastest flying mount at approximately 3300 speed. Hartalis is the fastest ground mount at approximately 2400 speed. Neptilius is the fastest water mount at approximately 2200 speed. All three are new or significantly upgraded in 1.0.

### Can I use combat Pals for base work?

Some combat Pals have decent Work Suitability, but specialists always outperform generalists in 1.0 due to the Level 10 system. Anubis is the exception — it's both a top-tier combat Pal and a strong handiwork worker. For everything else, keep separate combat and base teams.

### Is breeding worth it in 1.0?

Absolutely. The Mutation system and reduced Condensation requirements (48 instead of 116) make breeding more rewarding than ever. Start with Jormuntide Ignis — it's breedable from mid-game and remains viable through endgame content.
