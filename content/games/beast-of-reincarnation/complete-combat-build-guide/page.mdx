**Beast of Reincarnation** is Game Freak's first major non-Pokémon project, and it is nothing like the creature-catching comfort zone the studio is famous for. It is a fast, parry-heavy action RPG set in a post-apocalyptic Japan two thousand years in the future, starring an emotionless swordswoman named Emma and her very good boy, Koo. If you just picked it up on Xbox Game Pass or bought the Deluxe Edition, the skill tree can feel overwhelming, the combat punishes button-mashing, and the progression system branches in every direction. ## The Core Combat Loop: Parry First, Everything Else Follows

Beast of Reincarnation does not reward button-mashing. The entire combat system is built around a single idea: **read the enemy, parry at the right moment, then spend the resources you just earned.** Once you internalize this loop, the rest of the game becomes manageable.

Here is the basic loop in practice:

1. **Basic Attack** (R1 / RB / LMB) — your bread-and-butter. You press it to reduce HP and, more importantly, fill the enemy's **Down Gauge** (the stagger bar beneath their health).
2. **Parry** (L1 / LB / Q) — press just before an attack lands. A successful parry gives you a signature ringing sound, increases your **Entanglement Gauge**, builds Koo's **FP (Floresence Points)**, and rapidly fills the enemy's Down Gauge.
3. **Entanglement Gauge** — a yellow-and-blue resource bar. Parries fill it; Guarding drains it. You spend it on Blade Finishers and Nushi Skills.
4. **Down Gauge / Takedown** — once the enemy's stagger bar is full, they become vulnerable to a massive **Takedown** attack that deals far more damage than anything else in your kit.
5. **Koo's Bloom Arts** (Triangle / Y / F) — spend FP to command Koo. Higanbana: Roots binds enemies. Sakura: Javelin is a ranged nuke. Time slows when you open the menu, so you never have to panic-pick.

<CommonMistake title="Spamming Guard instead of Parrying">
Holding Guard costs Entanglement Gauge charges, and if the gauge runs out, Emma enters a vulnerable state where she takes increased damage. On Hard difficulty this is fatal. Parrying is free and actually rewards you. The timing window is generous enough that you should practice parrying every non-red attack.
</CommonMistake>

From my experience with parry-heavy action RPGs, the timing window in Beast of Reincarnation sits closer to **Sekiro** than to Stellar Blade — generous enough that you won't rage-quit, tight enough that mastery feels earned. [GamesRadar called boss fights](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/action-rpg/beast-of-reincarnation-review/) "a rhythm game where you learn to parry to the tune of each enemy's unique attack pattern," and that is exactly the right mindset.

## Perfect Dodge, Red Attacks, and When to Run

Not every attack can be parried. When an enemy flashes **red**, that means a **Killer Move** is coming. You cannot block or parry these. You must **Perfect Dodge** (Circle / B / Ctrl) at the last second. If you nail the timing, "Avoid" appears on screen and you can counter-attack immediately.

Some enemies also use **Brutal Moves** (blue flash). These can technically be blocked, but they drain a massive amount of your Entanglement Gauge. The correct answer is still to parry. If you parry a Brutal Move, you automatically trigger a **Ghostep** (teleport behind the enemy for a backstab).

## Emma's Skill Tree: The First 20 Hours

Your early-game build is built around one thing: **parrying.** Everything else flows from that. The three skills you need before anything else are **Iaido**, **Roll**, and **Helm Splitter**. Getting all three requires hitting specific attribute thresholds:

- **9 Strength** — unlocks Entanglement Overdrive and its sub-skills.
- **9 Florescence** — unlocks the Track Shot tree.
- **9 Vitality** — unlocks the Hair tree.

## Koo & Bloom Arts: FP Management

Most players treat Koo as a support afterthought. That is a mistake. Koo is half your damage output and most of your crowd control.

**Full Bloom** is Koo's super-state. When he enters it, he gets **100% extra damage** and **50% more stagger power**. You should not burn FP as soon as the bar is full; you should wait for Full Bloom, then unleash your most expensive art.

Koo's best early unlock is **Status Effect Amp** (7 Florescence). It makes status effects build faster, and since bosses stagger when a status lands, it directly creates more damage windows for Emma.

After that, get to **9 Strength** for **Taunt** and **Detect Hidden Item**, then grab **Mod: Resonance** at **7 Vitality**. Resonance causes the linked Bloom Art to recover Emma's Entanglement Gauge on use, which feeds directly back into your Iaido and Kiku combo pressure.

## Two Starting Builds That Work

### Build A: The Critical Parry Build (Recommended)

This is the most popular community build for a reason. It scales crit rate from Spirit Stones and turns every parry into a burst window.

- **Main Skill:** Entanglement Overdrive
- **Stat Priority:** ① Strength → ② Florescence → ③ Vitality
- **Weapon:** Cirrus Sword (Impact) early; Shark Cutter (crit on parry) mid-game; swap to elemental weapons that match boss weaknesses for boss fights
- **Armor:** Light Gambeson (5 Spirit Stone slots) → Floral Wraps
- **Spirit Stones:** Florescent Recovery (+6% Crit Rate), any Entangled variant (Assassination, Parry, Dodge), Onslaught
- **Bloom Arts:** Higanbana: Roots with Status Effect Amp, Sakura: Javelin with Resonance

<ProTip title="Farm Blighted Spirit Stones in the Crater Region">
Once you reach the Wasteland Region, head to the Crater Zone. Blighted Spirit Stones drop from elite enemies here and have higher base stats with automatically maxed unique effects. They are the best upgrade for your entire second half of the game.
</ProTip>

### Build B: The Stealth Skirmisher

If you prefer avoiding fights until you are ready, this build uses stealth and ranged attacks to thin groups before engaging.

- **Main Skill:** Hair (Cloaking Grass)
- **Stat Priority:** 9 Strength, 9 Florescence, 9 Vitality, then focus on Vitality for sustain
- **Weapon:** Zangetsu Bow or Hunter Sword (poison)
- **Armor:** Garb of Tranquility (low enemy awareness, 4 slots, 60 Acid Resist)
- **Playstyle:** Assassinate isolated targets, use ranged weapons to pick off stragglers, deploy Cloaking Grass when the group is too large

I recommend **Build A** for first-time players. The stealth approach is viable but slower, and the game does not have a traditional level-scaling system — skipping fights means missing crafting materials that make later bosses significantly easier.

## Boss Strategy Blueprint

Boss fights in Beast of Reincarnation are more about **demonstrating knowledge of the combat system** than pure DPS checks. Here is a universal framework:

1. **Learn the parry pattern first.** Every boss has a unique attack cadence. Spend the first 30 seconds of the fight watching, not attacking.
2. **Parry until the Down Gauge fills.** Do not burn your Entanglement charges on Blade Finishers until the stagger is close.
3. **Apply a status with Koo's Higanbana: Roots** as soon as the boss staggers. This extends the damage window.
4. **Dump the combo:** RB → RB → X → X + Finisher: Kiku (the Kiku Chaser combo). This is your primary boss damage tool once unlocked.
5. **Save Sakura: Javelin for Full Bloom.** When Koo is glowing, the nuke hits twice as hard.

## Five Common Beginner Mistakes

<CommonMistake title="Ignoring Koo's Full Bloom timing">
Burning FP the moment the bar is full wastes the 100% damage and 50% stagger bonus. Wait for the glow, then deploy your most expensive art.
</CommonMistake>

<CommonMistake title="Greedy skill investment">
Dumping all points into Strength feels good until you realize you cannot unlock Roll or Helm Splitter. The 9/9/9 threshold is a hard gate — plan for it.
</CommonMistake>

<CommonMistake title="Fighting every enemy in a group">
The game is balanced around thinning the herd first. Use ranged attacks, stealth kills, and Higanbana: Roots to reduce numbers before you commit to melee.
</CommonMistake>

<CommonMistake title="Skipping Dragon Vein Shrines">
These shrines (discovered starting in Chapter 2) increase your Stimpack capacity. Without them, you enter Chapter 3 with only three heals, which is not enough for the boss.
</CommonMistake>

<CommonMistake title="Not cooking new meals">
Every new recipe provides a stacking combat buff. Players who skip cooking reach the mid-game underpowered by a noticeable margin.
</CommonMistake>

## FAQ

**Can you respec in Beast of Reincarnation?**
No. There is no respec system in the current version (1.0.7). Every attribute point and skill point is permanent. That is why the 9/9/9 opening is so important — if you over-invest in one stat early, you lock yourself out of key skills until much later.

**How long is the game?**
A focused first playthrough is roughly 25–30 hours across 13 chapters and an epilogue. If you explore thoroughly, expect 40–50 hours. Earning every achievement requires a second playthrough in New Game+ and takes 60–70 hours total.

**What difficulty should I start on?**
Normal. Story difficulty is viable if you only care about the narrative, but it does not teach you the combat system. Hard is punishing for a first playthrough because repeated failed attempts burn through your limited Stimpack supply. Normal is the sweet spot for learning parry timing while still feeling challenging.

**Is there multiplayer or co-op?**
No. Beast of Reincarnation is strictly single-player. The only companion is Koo, and he is AI-controlled.

**What is the best early weapon?**
The **Cirrus Sword** (Impact) is the best all-rounder for the first three chapters. Its Clear Skies ability boosts both Emma and Koo's damage by up to 7% for 15 seconds after a successful parry, which synergizes perfectly with the parry-first build.

## Final Verdict: Start Parrying, Start Winning

After parsing the Game8 wiki and cross-referencing with VGC's chapter data, my biggest recommendation is this: **treat the first two chapters as a parry tutorial.** The game does not punish you for being slow; it punishes you for being greedy. Invest the 9/9/9 attributes, unlock Iaido and Roll as fast as possible, and let Koo's Bloom Arts do the crowd control while you focus on the boss.
