## Your First Hour: What to Do and in What Order

Once the game unlocks, resist the urge to button-mash through the opening. Here's the priority list that'll save you an hour of backtracking:

### 1. Pick Your Difficulty Honestly

Three modes are available from the start: **Story** (narrative-focused, forgiving combat), **Standard** (the intended experience), and **Hard** (tighter parry windows, aggressive AI, resource scarcity). You can switch mid-campaign, so there's no wrong choice — but if action RPGs aren't your usual genre, start on Story. The combat is timing-based and punishing if you treat it like a button-masher.

### 2. Learn the Parry System Before Anything Else

This is the single most important thing to internalize. Beast of Reincarnation uses a Sekiro-like deflection system — perfect parries give you **zero chip damage, massive stagger buildup, and a Blight Energy refund**. Regular blocks eat 30% chip damage and drain stamina fast.

The parry window sits at roughly 8 frames at 60 FPS. It's tight but learnable. Pro tip from the community: initiate a slight movement before raising your guard — something about the animation blending makes the timing feel more consistent.

### 3. Start Commanding Koo Immediately

Koo isn't a pet that auto-attacks while you do the real work. He's a co-protagonist with four commands on cooldowns:

- **Distracting Bark** — draws aggro for 5 seconds (15s cooldown)
- **Hamstring Bite** — 40% enemy slow (20s cooldown)
- **Sealer's Howl** — 15% damage buff for Emma (45s cooldown)
- **Flora Sniff** — highlights hidden healing plants (60s cooldown)

The combat loop is built around alternating between Emma's parries and Koo's commands. The command menu slows time without pausing — use it as a tactical breather. Players who ignore Koo are fighting at half strength.

### 4. Understand Camps and Checkpoints

Camps work like bonfires in Dark Souls: resting restores health and respawns enemies. Always bank your resources (spirit stones, Amber) before resting. Sometimes pushing forward to the next camp is smarter than backtracking to a safe one.

One thing that sets Beast of Reincarnation apart from most soulslike games: **there's no death penalty.** You don't lose experience or currency on death. You just respawn at the last camp, which makes exploration feel far less stressful.

### 5. Explore the Blight Forests Early

Emma's plant-vine powers are your traversal toolkit — double-jumping, grappling to zip-lines, and creating vine bridges. The blight forests that spawn around Nushi territories are the game's most hostile zones, but they're also where the best loot lives: spirit stones, rare crafting materials, and optional bosses that drop unique charms for Koo.

If a vine path looks possible, it almost always leads somewhere.
