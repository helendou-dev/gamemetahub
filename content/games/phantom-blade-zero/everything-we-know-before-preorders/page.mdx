S-GAME dropped a bomb on August 6: Phantom Blade Zero is officially gold. Development is complete, pre-orders open on **August 12 at 10:00 AM Beijing Time** (August 11 at 7:00 PM PT), and the game launches **October 29, 2026** on PS5 and PC. Alongside pre-orders, we're getting an 11-minute trailer with new story content, characters, combat footage, and what the studio cryptically calls "something unexpected."

I've been tracking this game since its reveal at the May 2023 PlayStation Showcase, and after getting hands-on with it at Summer Game Fest 2026, I'm cautiously optimistic. Here's everything you need to know before pre-orders go live.

## What Is Phantom Blade Zero?

You play as **Soul**, an elite assassin serving an organization called The Order. He gets framed for murdering the patriarch, barely survives, and a mysterious healer patches him up — but only buys him **66 days to live**. The setup is essentially a death timer narrative: every quest, every side activity, every boss fight carries weight because Soul's clock is ticking.

The world blends traditional Chinese wuxia with steampunk and cyberpunk aesthetics — a style S-GAME calls **"Kungfupunk."** Think rain-soaked ancient courtyards with mechanical prosthetics and steam-powered weaponry. Creator Soulframe Liang has cited [Hong Kong martial arts cinema and Hung Ga kung fu](https://gamingtrend.com/previews/phantom-blade-zero-successfully-straddles-the-line-between-soulslike-and-action-rpg) as core inspirations, and it shows in every frame of gameplay footage.

## The Combat System Is the Real Star

This is where I need to be honest: I went into my SGF demo expecting a Sekiro clone. I was wrong. Phantom Blade Zero plays like nothing else on the market right now.

The core resource is **Sha-Chi** — a dual-purpose energy gauge that powers both your offense and defense. Light attacks build it, heavy attacks and blocks spend it. When your Sha-Chi runs dry, you're defenseless. I learned this the hard way during my demo when I got greedy with heavy attacks on a spear-wielding mini-boss and ended up stun-locked with zero Sha-Chi, unable to block the follow-up combo. That moment taught me the entire game's rhythm in about three seconds.

The weapon variety is staggering — **30+ primary blades** (swords, greatswords, flexible swords, chakrams) and **20+ Phantom Edge secondaries** (bows, axes, cannons, hammers, throwing blades). Each primary has its own skill tree and upgrade path. I tried about 10 weapons in my 30-minute demo, and every single one felt mechanically distinct. The dual daggers were fast and built Sha-Chi quickly but had terrible range. The greatsword hit like a truck but left me wide open if I whiffed. My favorite? A flexible sword that could whip around shields — felt like cheating until a boss caught me mid-animation and deleted half my health bar.

## What the 11-Minute Trailer Should Reveal

According to [S-GAME's official announcement on X](https://x.com/pbzero_official), the August 12 trailer will showcase new story content, characters, combat, and "something unexpected." Based on what was teased at the June State of Play, I expect we'll see:

- **Mu Xiaokui** — daughter of The Order's founder, shown in the 35-second teaser departing in what looks like a hot-air balloon. She's almost certainly a major story character, possibly an antagonist or uneasy ally.
- **New combat mechanics** — the SGF demo only showed basic combat. I'm betting we'll see advanced Ghoststep chains and Power Surge combos in action.
- **Boss encounters** — the demo had mini-bosses but no full boss fights. The 11-minute runtime suggests we'll get at least one major showcase.
- **Price and PC specs** — S-GAME confirmed pricing and hardware requirements will be revealed alongside pre-orders.

## Should You Pre-Order?

Here's where I give you a straight answer: **if you loved Sekiro or Ninja Gaiden, pre-order without hesitation.** This is the most promising martial arts action RPG I've played since Nioh 2, and the combat depth already feels comparable to genre titans.

However — and this is a big however — I have two concerns. First, the SGF build had noticeable frame rate drops during heavy particle effects. S-GAME delayed the game from September 9 to October 29 specifically to polish these issues, so I'm hopeful, but I'd be lying if I said I wasn't watching closely. Second, we still don't know how the semi-open world holds up outside of the demo's linear path. If the exploration and side quest content is as thin as it was in some recent action RPGs, the 20-30 hour main story could feel padded.

**My recommendation:** pre-order on PC if your hardware can handle it (specs drop August 12), but if you're on PS5, wait for the digital pre-order reviews to confirm performance is locked. The game is PS5 console-exclusive for 12 months, so there's no Xbox version to fall back on.

If you're new to the genre entirely, skip the pre-order and pick it up at launch — start on Story difficulty, not Sha-Chi Master. I watched multiple people at SGF bounce off the combat in five minutes because they picked the hardest setting and got wrecked by the first mini-boss. Don't be that person.

Phantom Blade Zero is shaping up to be one of the most important releases of 2026. After Black Myth: Wukong proved Chinese AAA can compete globally, S-GAME has a chance to prove it wasn't a one-off. The 11-minute trailer on August 12 will tell us a lot — I'll be watching at 10:00 AM Beijing Time, and you should too.
