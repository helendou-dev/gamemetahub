**Phantom Blade Zero pre-orders are now officially live** — S-GAME flipped the switch at 7:00 PM PT on August 11 across PS5, Steam, Epic Games Store, and WeGame, and confirmed the long-awaited pricing alongside an 11-minute gameplay trailer.

## Confirmed pricing: $59.99 Standard, $69.99 Deluxe

The wait is over — S-GAME confirmed both editions today:

| Edition | Price (USD) | Includes |
|---------|------------|----------|
| Standard | **$59.99** | Full game + preorder bonuses |
| Deluxe | **$69.99** | Full game + preorder bonuses + 2 exclusive outfits + digital artbook + digital soundtrack |

The $59.99 Standard undercuts the usual $69.99 AAA norm and lands below the rumored figure the creative director publicly called too high. The Deluxe's $10 premium buys the "Wulin Legend" and "Blood-Steel Form" outfits plus the digital artbook and soundtrack — store pages are flipping from wishlist to pre-order right now, so check your platform for regional pricing.

## Preorder bonuses — all obtainable in-game

S-GAME confirmed the preorder bonuses are **early-access cosmetics, not exclusive content**:

- **Treasure Basin** (聚宝盆): equippable accessory that boosts Remnant Dust drop rate by 10%
- **Legacy** (传承): exclusive outfit
- **Deluxe extras**: "Wulin Legend" and "Blood-Steel Form" outfits, digital artbook, digital soundtrack

Nothing is locked behind preordering — every item can be unlocked through normal gameplay, which makes this a low-FOMO preorder. You're paying for early access to cosmetics and, on PS5, day-one preload convenience.

## The 11-minute trailer is out

The new footage — promised to cover new story content, previously unseen characters, expanded combat, and "something unexpected" — dropped alongside the preorder launch. If you're on the fence, **watch the trailer first, then decide**: the price won't move for months.

## The road here

Phantom Blade Zero was originally targeting September 9, but S-GAME pushed the date to October 29 to polish character models and performance. That delay now looks vindicated: development is complete eight weeks ahead of launch, the game has gone gold, and the studio's 1.62 million Steam wishlists — a record for a Chinese-developed single-player title — are finally converting into purchases. Player sentiment on launch day is overwhelmingly "day-one buy," with the main discussion being price, which S-GAME has now settled.

## What to check before you buy

- **PC specs are official now**: GTX 1660 minimum / RTX 3060 Ti recommended, with a mandatory NVMe SSD — and the game lists **Denuvo DRM**
- **Platforms**: PS5 console-exclusive for at least 12 months; PC on Steam, Epic, and WeGame
- **Release date**: October 29, 2026 — development is complete and the game has gone gold
