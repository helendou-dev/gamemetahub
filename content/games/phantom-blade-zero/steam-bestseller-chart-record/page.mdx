**Phantom Blade Zero didn't just open pre-orders today — it immediately stormed to #1 on Steam's global Top Sellers chart**, beating out free-to-play behemoths like Counter-Strike 2, Apex Legends, and Dota 2 within hours of the pre-order switch flipping on.

That a $59.99 paid pre-order is outselling free games is the kind of market signal you rarely see, and it validates the 1.62 million Steam wishlists that S-GAME has been sitting on since December 2025. The wishlist-to-purchase conversion is happening in real time.

## The numbers behind the chart-topping

According to [Steam Top Sellers data from Datahumble](https://datahumble.com/top-sellers), Phantom Blade Zero sat at **#1 globally** as of August 12, 2026, with Counter-Strike 2, Apex Legends, Overwatch, and Big Walk rounding out the top five. The chart ranks by revenue, not units — meaning a paid game at $59.99 needs roughly 60x fewer transactions than a free-to-play title's microtransactions to claim the same spot.

Chinese social media erupted with the news. A Weibo post by user @厄加特特 accumulated thousands of reposts with the hashtag #影之刃零登顶全球畅销榜#, noting that the game hit #1 in both the global and China regional charts simultaneously — a first for a Chinese-developed single-player title.

## What's driving the surge

Three factors converged today:

- **Price reveal at $59.99** — S-GAME landed below the $69.99 AAA norm, a move the creative director had publicly pushed for. The Deluxe Edition at $69.99 adds two exclusive outfits, a digital artbook, and the soundtrack. Our [complete preorder guide](/games/phantom-blade-zero/preorder-guide) breaks down which edition is worth your money.
- **The 11-minute trailer** — new story content, expanded combat footage, and the reveal of Donnie Yen as both creative consultant and the face of Mo Yuan, the protagonist's father. S-GAME's stunt team brought Hong Kong martial arts cinema choreography directly into the motion capture pipeline.
- **Development is complete** — the game went gold, and the October 29 launch date is locked. No more delays.

## The bigger picture: Chinese AAA's commercial moment

Two years ago, Black Myth: Wukong proved a Chinese studio could ship a globally competitive AAA single-player game. Phantom Blade Zero topping the Steam revenue chart on pre-order day — not launch day, *pre-order day* — suggests that wasn't a one-off. The market is ready to pay upfront for Chinese-developed action RPGs at Western AAA price points.

S-GAME has Tencent and Sony backing, 1.62 million wishlists converting at a rate that put a paid game above every free-to-play live service on Steam, and a dedicated [State of Play on August 17](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/08/phantom-blade-zero-pre-orders-now-available-11-minute-trailer) that will show 15-20 minutes of new gameplay. If you missed the pre-order news this morning, [here's everything that went live](/games/phantom-blade-zero/pre-orders-trailer-august-2026).

The August 17 State of Play is the next inflection point. If S-GAME shows a polished, deep combat system — not just flashy trailers — expect the chart position to hold through launch.
