Phantom Blade Zero pre-orders have been live since **August 11 at 7:00 PM PT**, and with a dedicated State of Play landing **this Monday, August 17**, this is the busiest week in the game's marketing cycle. If you're trying to decide whether to buy now, wait for the deep dive, or skip it entirely, here are the quick answers — no fluff.

## When does Phantom Blade Zero come out?

**October 29, 2026** on PlayStation 5 and PC (Steam, Epic Games Store, and WeGame). Development is complete and the game has gone gold — the launch date is locked.

The $59.99 Standard undercuts the $69.99 AAA default — S-GAME's creative director pushed for it and delivered. The Deluxe's $10 premium buys two exclusive outfits plus the art book and soundtrack; it's a cosmetics-and-collector upsell, not content.

## What are the pre-order bonuses?

Pre-ordering **either** edition unlocks two items early:

- **Treasure Basin** (accessory): +10% Remnant Dust drop rate — a farming QoL item, not a combat advantage
- **Legacy** (outfit): a full character costume

The honest part: **both items are also obtainable through normal in-game progression.** There's no paid early access, no exclusive weapons, no FOMO content cuts. Pre-ordering only gets you these cosmetics ahead of schedule.

## Is Phantom Blade Zero a PS5 exclusive?

The PS5 version is a **console exclusive for at least 12 months** — no Xbox or Switch 2 version is confirmed. PC is fully supported via Steam, Epic, and WeGame from day one, so the exclusivity only applies to consoles.

## Does it have Denuvo?

Yes. Both the Steam and Epic pages list **Denuvo Anti-Tamper DRM**. If that's a dealbreaker (performance overhead, Linux/Steam Deck compatibility issues), factor it in now — and remember Steam's 2-hour refund window is your safety net if DRM misbehaves on your hardware.

## What happens at the August 17 State of Play?

Sony is giving Phantom Blade Zero its own dedicated State of Play — a rarity reserved for games the platform believes in (the last solo one was Astro Bot). It streams **August 17 at 7:00 PM PT / 10:00 PM ET** on PlayStation's Twitch and YouTube channels, and runs **nearly 20 minutes of pure gameplay** — not trailers.

S-GAME founder Soulframe Liang confirmed the deep dive will introduce never-before-seen characters central to the story and show how expressive player builds can be. This is the "is it more than stylish trailers?" moment. One practical note: Sony warns the broadcast includes licensed music that may flag co-streams and VOD archives on YouTube.

## Should you pre-order before or after the State of Play?

**If you're on PC:** the smart play is wait. The State of Play is three days away and will show the combat and build systems in actual detail — exactly the information you'd want before committing $59.99. Steam pre-orders also don't lock you into anything you can't refund later.

**If you're on PS5 and already sold:** pre-ordering now gets you the bonus cosmetics and day-one preload, and the bonuses aren't exclusive content, so there's no downside beyond the $59.99 price itself.

**If you're undecided on the combat:** Phantom Blade Zero is not a Soulslike. It's character-action built on the Sha-Chi resource system, weapon-swap combos, and Ghoststep parry counters. The State of Play is the decision point — watch it, then buy.
