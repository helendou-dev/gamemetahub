## Price: Confirmed and Still Pending

S-GAME revealed pricing **with the preorder launch at 7:00 PM PT on August 11**. Here's where things stand:

- **Standard Edition: $59.99 on Steam** — confirmed via the US Steam storefront at launch. That undercuts the usual $69.99 AAA norm, and the creative director had publicly said the higher rumored figure was too expensive.
- **Deluxe Edition: $69.99** — confirmed by S-GAME's official announcement (also £64.99 / €79.99). The Deluxe adds two exclusive outfits ("Wulin Legend" and "Blood-Steel Form") plus a digital artbook and soundtrack; check your platform for its regional price.
- **PS5 physical copies** are listed at $69.99 by retailers like Play-Asia, but S-GAME issued a statement in May asking players to ignore circulating physical-edition price listings, so treat disc pricing as unconfirmed.

Caveat: a third-party "buying guide" this week claims editions at $69.99/$89.99/$199.99 with a "Gilded Executioner" skin pack and "72-hour early access" — **none of that is official**. S-GAME has only confirmed a single one-time-purchase model with no paid early access, and the actual Standard price ($59.99) is lower than that guide's claim. Ignore unverified edition lists until the official store pages update.

**My take**: at $59.99, that's a fair price for a 20–30 hour main campaign. The Deluxe's $10 premium for two outfits plus artbook and soundtrack is a reasonable upsell if you collect cosmetics — otherwise the Standard is the value pick — more below.

## Preorder Bonuses: What You Actually Get

The Steam page has confirmed the preorder bonuses — and here's the refreshing part: **they're cosmetic-and-QoL, not content.**

- **Treasure Basin (聚宝盆) accessory**: +10% Remnant Dust drop rate
- **Legacy (传承) outfit**: a character costume

Both are described as **also obtainable through normal gameplay** — preordering just unlocks them early. No paid early access, no exclusive weapons, no FOMO content. That's a player-respectful choice, and it makes the preorder decision purely about price and platform.

## The Denuvo Warning

Both the Steam and Epic pages list **Denuvo Anti-Tamper DRM**. If Denuvo is a dealbreaker (performance overhead, Linux/Steam Deck compatibility issues are common), factor that in now. Steam Deck owners should check compatibility reports before buying — and the 2-hour Steam refund window is your safety net if the DRM misbehaves on your setup.

## Which Platform Should You Preorder On?

**PS5 if**: you want the DualSense experience — adaptive triggers and haptics genuinely elevate parry-and-counter combat — and you're fine with a console-only library. Sony's exclusivity deal also makes PS5 the priority optimization target.

**Steam if**: you're on PC and want the safest buy. The 2-hour refund window lets you stress-test performance and Denuvo compatibility on your exact hardware. The 1.62 million Steam wishlists (a record for a Chinese-developed single-player title) also mean a rich community ecosystem on day one.

**Epic Games Store if**: you play Fortnite — the Phantom Blade Zero × Fortnite crossover (teased by Epic's August 7 "Lone Wolf" post under the Gaming Legends banner) means EGS preorders may bundle exclusive Fortnite cosmetics. Otherwise, Steam wins for a single-player game.

**WeGame if**: you're in China — best regional pricing and localization support.

## The 11-Minute Trailer: Watch Before You Decide

The trailer drops as preorders open and covers new story content, previously unseen characters, expanded combat footage, and what S-GAME calls "something unexpected" — widely expected to be the Fortnite crossover reveal or a new game mode. If you're on the fence, **watch it first, then buy**.

## Should You Preorder at All?

**Yes, preorder if:**
- You've been locked in since the 2023 PlayStation Showcase reveal and want the preorder cosmetics plus day-one preload on PS5
- The confirmed $59.99 price lands where you'd pay at launch anyway — preordering is then pure convenience

**Wait if:**
- You're on PC and want real-world benchmarks — Denuvo overhead and upscaling quality on *your* GPU are unknowns until launch
- You're undecided on the combat. It's not a Soulslike — it's character-action built on the Sha-Chi resource system, Ghoststep counters, and weapon-swap combos. Watch the trailer, or the State of Play deep dive expected late summer
- Your backlog is real. It's a single-player game with no time-limited content — it will be there in January

**Don't preorder if:**
- You think you're "supporting the developers." S-GAME is backed by Tencent and Sony — the 1.62 million wishlists and WeGame's 100k reservations in three days already proved demand
- Denuvo is a dealbreaker for you

**Bottom line**: the confirmed specs are player-friendly, the bonuses are honest (no content cuts, no early access), and the Standard price came in below the AAA norm at $59.99. At that price, this is a low-risk preorder for anyone who wants the cosmetics. If the Deluxe lands higher than you'd like, the safe play is wishlist → watch the trailer → decide at launch-day reviews.
