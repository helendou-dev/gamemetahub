Diablo IV is coming to Nintendo Switch 2 on **Tuesday, September 15, 2026** — a launch date announced at BlizzCon on Saturday and arriving just three days later, one of the tightest reveal-to-release windows in the franchise's history. The port makes Switch 2 the sixth platform for Blizzard's open-world action RPG, and the first Nintendo hardware to ever host a live-service Diablo.

Here is everything you need to know before the eShop unlocks.

---

## What You Get for $69.99

The Switch 2 release comes as the **Age of Hatred Collection**, a single SKU priced at **$69.99** (£62.99 / €69.99, with Japan at ¥8,400 including tax on September 16). The bundle includes:

- **Diablo IV** — the full base game and Lilith campaign
- **Vessel of Hatred** — the first expansion, adding the Nahantu jungle region and the Spiritborn class
- **Lord of Hatred** — the second expansion, moving the story to the Isles of Skovos with the Paladin and Warlock classes

That is eight playable classes in total on day one: Barbarian, Druid, Necromancer, Rogue, Sorcerer, Spiritborn, Paladin, and Warlock. Blizzard has also confirmed an Amazon class pack arriving in the first half of 2027.

There is **no base-game-only option** on Switch 2 and no announced upgrade path for players who already own Diablo IV on PC or other consoles. If you are double-dipping, you are buying the full collection again.

---

## Cross-Progression: The Big Win for Existing Players

The part that matters most for anyone with an existing Battle.net account: **full cross-play and cross-progression are confirmed**. Your characters from PC, PlayStation, or Xbox carry over to Switch 2 once your Battle.net account is linked — paragon levels, stash, cosmetics, and seasonal progress all follow you.

That makes the Switch 2 version a genuine "couch and commute" companion rather than a restart. Blizzard demonstrated a playable build at BlizzCon, and attendee reports on the handheld mode's responsiveness were positive, though official resolution and frame-rate targets have not been published yet.

## Four Switch-Specific Things to Check Before Buying

1. **The physical edition has no cartridge.** Retail copies are code-in-box — you are downloading the entire game either way.
2. **Budget your storage.** The file size is unconfirmed for Switch 2, but Diablo IV runs around 80 GB on other platforms. With 256 GB of internal storage, a microSD Express card is a sensible precaution.
3. **You need Nintendo Switch Online.** The eShop listing confirms online features, cross-play included, require an active NSO membership plus a Nintendo Account — on top of Diablo IV's always-online requirement on every platform.
4. **Switch 2 owners get a small head start.** The version ships with 14 character slots and 7 stash tabs from the start, plus three unlockable World of Warcraft cosmetic items.

---

## Season 15 Lands the Same Day

Switch 2 players will not be waiting on the seasonal treadmill. **Season 15, "Season of Hell's Legacy,"** begins rolling out on **September 15 at 9:30 AM PT** — hours before most players have even finished installing.

The season celebrates the Diablo series' 30th anniversary with a questline built around echoes of Diablo, Baal, and Mephisto, plus multi-level Waking Nightmares dungeons and Soul Splinters — a new gem slot type that trades a bonus for a drawback. Nintendo players start their first seasonal character on the same footing as everyone else.

## What to Watch After Launch

Performance numbers in both handheld and docked modes are the last unknown — we will know within hours of the September 15 unlock how the Switch 2 build holds up in Nahantu's densest fights. Beyond that, Blizzard used the same BlizzCon stage to confirm Diablo V for spring 2029, which makes this collection the definitive way to play the current era of Sanctuary on Nintendo hardware for the next three years.
